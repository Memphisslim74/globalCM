Giving questions

Frequently asked questions.

Is my donation tax-deductible?

The Global Co-Mission Inc. is a registered U.S. 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Donations are tax-deductible in full or in part where allowed; treatment may vary by country. Tax ID: 93-3384807.

Where does my donation go?

Unless you designate a project, your gift supports the General Fund so resources can be directed to the ministry’s greatest current needs. Designated gifts are applied to the named campaign whenever possible.

Do you accept in-kind donations?

In-kind gifts may be considered based on the item or service. GCOM works remotely and uses a trusted mail-forwarding provider, helping reduce overhead and focus capacity on the mission.

Can I give in honor or memory of someone?

Yes. Memorial gifts can honor a loved one while supporting Gospel campaigns and ongoing outreach. Contact us if you need help coordinating a tribute gift.
