About The Global Co-Mission

Preparing the way for a global Jubilee.

We rally churches to collaborate in holistic, city-wide, open-air Gospel campaigns that proclaim salvation, freedom, and restoration in Jesus.

Our mission

One Name. One Gospel. Churches together.

We build strategic, community-specific partnerships that help prepare the way for the Lord and strengthen the local church before, during, and after each campaign.

Our vision is a global family of churches standing across denominations around one shared passion: the Gospel preached. Together, we ignite a Global Jubilee.
